The Marks & Standards says the official GPA method is on Moodle, so this tool uses a simple weighted average: Σ(weight × GPA) ÷ Σ(weights), over entered results only. This shows Year 2 only. Your Year 1 GPA also feeds the aggregate GPA at the end of Year 2, which this page doesn't calculate. Always check your official transcript.
